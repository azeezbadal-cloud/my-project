# How to put My Magic Busy Book on Shopify — step by step

Time needed: about 1 hour. You need: a computer, your Shopify login, and the files from the chat.

---

## STEP 0 — Get the files ready on your computer

1. Download these files from the chat and save them in one folder (for example "Busy Book"):
   - `Magic_Busy_Book_US_Letter.pdf`
   - `Magic_Busy_Book_A4.pdf`
   - `Start_Here_Printing_Guide.pdf`
   - `Magic_Busy_Book_Customer_Files.zip` (backup option)
   - `Little_Worlds_My_Magic_Busy_Book_PUBLISHER.zip`
2. Unzip (double-click) the **PUBLISHER** zip. Inside, open the folder `06_SHOPIFY`. You will use these files:
   - `Cover.png`
   - `Product_Image_1_Cover.png` … `Product_Image_5_Whats_Included.png`
   - `Product_Description.html`
3. **Never** upload anything from the PUBLISHER folder to customers. Customers only get the 3 PDFs (or the customer ZIP).

---

## STEP 1 — Quick check before selling (15–30 min)

1. Open `Magic_Busy_Book_US_Letter.pdf` and print **page 5** and **page 41**.
2. In the print window choose **"Actual size"** or **"Scale 100%"** (NOT "Fit to page").
3. Cut out 2–3 cards from page 41 and put them on the dashed boxes on page 5. They should fit inside the boxes.
4. Flip through the PDF once to look for anything you want to change.

---

## STEP 2 — Install the free Digital Downloads app

1. Log in to Shopify admin (your-store.myshopify.com/admin).
2. In the left menu click **Apps** → **Shopify App Store** (or visit apps.shopify.com).
3. Search **"Digital Downloads"** (made by Shopify). Click **Install**.

---

## STEP 3 — Create the product

1. Left menu → **Products** → **Add product**.
2. **Title** — copy and paste:

   ```
   My Magic Busy Book — 36 Interactive Preschool Activities | Printable PDF
   ```

3. **Description**
   - In the description box, click the **`<>` (Show HTML)** button.
   - Open `06_SHOPIFY/Product_Description.html` with Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac), select all, copy, and paste it into the box.
   - Click `<>` again to see the normal view. It should show headings and bullet points.

4. **Media** — click **Upload new** and choose, in this order:
   1. `Cover.png` (this becomes the main picture)
   2. `Product_Image_1_Cover.png`
   3. `Product_Image_2_Open_Book_Mockup.png`
   4. `Product_Image_3_Board_And_Pieces.png`
   5. `Product_Image_4_Worlds_Collage.png`
   6. `Product_Image_5_Whats_Included.png`

   Then click each image → **Add alt text** and paste the matching line:

   | Image | Alt text |
   |---|---|
   | Cover.png | My Magic Busy Book cover with Leo the lion cub cheering, animal and fruit cards, shapes and a number train, labelled Ages 3–5 |
   | Image 1 | Front cover of the My Magic Busy Book printable PDF on a cream background |
   | Image 2 | Digital mockup of two printed pages: the Rainbow Rescue colour-matching board and cut-out sheet 1 |
   | Image 3 | Rainbow Rescue board with six cloud spaces beside six cut-out raindrop cards in red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple |
   | Image 4 | Three pages from the book: Find My Home (Animal Island), Number Train (Number Town) and Dress for the Weather (Feelings Forest) |
   | Image 5 | What's included: 48 printable pages, 36 missions, 5 cut-out sheets, US Letter and A4 PDFs |

5. **Pricing** → Price: **9.99** (your store should be set to AUD; check under Settings → Store details if unsure).
6. **Inventory** → untick **"Track quantity"**.
7. **Shipping** → untick **"This is a physical product"**. (Very important — otherwise customers get charged shipping.)
8. **Product organization** (right side):
   - Product type: `Printable Activity Book`
   - Vendor: `Little Worlds Learning`
   - Tags — paste:

     ```
     busy book, printable busy book, preschool printables, ages 3-5, toddler activities, matching game, sorting activity, counting activities, numbers 1-10, colour matching, shapes, quiet book, screen-free play, homeschool preschool, early learning, fine motor, PDF download, US Letter, A4, Little Worlds Learning
     ```

9. **Search engine listing** (bottom of the page) → click **Edit**:
   - Page title:

     ```
     My Magic Busy Book – Printable Preschool Busy Book PDF
     ```

   - Meta description:

     ```
     48-page printable busy book for ages 3–5: 36 missions to match, sort, count and play. US Letter + A4 PDFs. Digital download, adult prep needed.
     ```

   - URL handle:

     ```
     my-magic-busy-book-printable-preschool-activities
     ```

10. **Status** (top right) → leave it as **Draft** for now.
11. Click **Save**.

---

## STEP 4 — Attach the files customers will download

1. Left menu → **Apps** → **Digital Downloads**.
2. Find **My Magic Busy Book** and click it (or on the product page use **More actions → Digital Downloads**).
3. Click **Add attachment / Upload file** and add:
   - `Magic_Busy_Book_US_Letter.pdf`
   - `Magic_Busy_Book_A4.pdf`
   - `Start_Here_Printing_Guide.pdf`
4. If it only allows **one** file, upload `Magic_Busy_Book_Customer_Files.zip` instead.
5. Save.

---

## STEP 5 — Test it like a customer

1. Settings → **Payments** → turn on **test mode** (Shopify Payments: "Enable test mode"), or create a 100% discount code under **Discounts**.
2. Set the product to **Active** (Step 6), open your store, buy the book with the test card / discount code.
3. Check: you get the order email with download links, and all 3 files open.
4. Turn test mode off afterwards.

---

## STEP 6 — Go live

1. Open the product → change **Status** from **Draft** to **Active** → **Save**.
2. Optional: Products → **Collections** → create "Printable Activity Books" and add the book.
3. Optional: add it to your homepage or menu (Online Store → Themes → Customize / Navigation).
4. Make sure your store has a **Contact** page and a **refund policy for digital products** (Settings → Policies).

---

## STEP 7 — Tell people (optional)

Everything is written and ready in the PUBLISHER folder → `07_MARKETING`:

- `Instagram_Captions.md` — 5 ready-to-post captions
- `Pinterest_Copy.md` — 5 pin titles + descriptions
- `Launch_Email.md` — email to send to your subscribers
- `Free_Sample_Activity.pdf` — give this away free to collect email sign-ups
- `Reel_Scripts.md` — 3 short video plans (film real printed pages)
