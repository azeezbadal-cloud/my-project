# Known Limitations – Leo's Little Lines v1.0

- **No physical print or KDP proof yet.** Order a KDP proof copy before publishing; print a few home pages at Actual size.
- **KDP spine width** is computed as 72 × 0.002252 in (white paper). Confirm in KDP's cover calculator; if KDP gives a different width, the cover must be regenerated (one setting in `tb_build.py`).
- **Standard colour availability** for 8.5 × 11 should be checked in your KDP marketplace; premium colour works but costs more per copy.
- Paperback pages are printed on both sides – the guide and listing recommend crayons or pencils, not markers.
- Stroke order follows one simple uppercase print style; schools in some regions teach slightly different formations.
- PPTX illustrations are transparent PNG pictures (SVG masters in 02_ILLUSTRATIONS); text, shapes and dotted lines are native. Canva import not tested.
- Letter pages show uppercase only (deliberate for ages 2–4).
- No contact details, reviews or awards were invented. Nothing was uploaded to Shopify or Amazon.
